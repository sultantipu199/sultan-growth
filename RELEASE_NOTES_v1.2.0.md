## 🚀 Key Deliverables & Features

### 1. New Dedicated Section: `#executive-assets`
- **Location:** Prominently situated between the Proof/Creative vault and the Teardown Audit section.
- **Categorized Tabs:** Quick filtering between `All Assets (4)`, `Curriculum Vitae (EN & AR)`, and `22-Slide Decks`.
- **4 Featured Executive Asset Cards:**
  1. **Tipu Sultan — Executive CV (English Edition)**: `.pdf` and `.docx` direct downloads + interactive modal preview.
  2. **تيبو سلطان — السيرة الذاتية التنفيذية (Arabic Edition)**: 3-page localized PDF direct download + interactive Arabic modal preview.
  3. **عروض محفظة الأعمال — 22 شريحة (Arabic Presentation Deck)**: Full 22-slide Gulf-market strategy deck `.pptx` (10.2 MB) + in-browser slide viewer.
  4. **Next-Gen Performance Architecture Deck (English Presentation Deck)**: Full 22-slide global DTC/SaaS strategy deck `.pptx` (10.3 MB) + in-browser slide viewer.
- **Supplementary Presentations Drawer:** Direct access to Google Ads Deck and Case Studies Volumes 1 & 2.

### 2. Interactive In-Browser CV Viewer Modal (`#cvModal`)
- **Bilingual Switcher:** Toggle between English (2 Pages) and Arabic (3 Pages) on the fly.
- **Page Tabs & Controls:** Dynamic page tabs (`P1: Summary`, `P2: Experience` / `ص 1: الملخص`, `ص 2: الخبرة`, `ص 3: الأدلة`) with Previous / Next buttons.
- **Direct Downloads:** One-click downloads for PDF and Word DOCX formats.
- **Executive Snapshot Sidebar:** Instant access to verified metrics ($188K+ spend, $869K+ revenue, 4.6x ROAS, 59 proofs) and WhatsApp availability trigger.

### 3. Interactive 22-Slide Presentation Viewer (`#deckModal`)
- **Full Slide Carousel:** All 22 slides with rich headlines, metric callouts, and structured takeaways.
- **Live Language Toggle:** Instant switch between English `[EN]` and Arabic `[AR]` presentations without losing slide position.
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Left/Right arrow keys (`←`, `→`) to flip slides, `ESC` to close.
- **Slide Strip Jumper:** 22 quick-jump number buttons for fast stakeholder inspection.

### 4. Navigation & Hero Upgrades
- **Desktop Navbar:** Added `CV & Decks` link with gold badge (`New`).
- **Mobile Navigation Drawer:** Added `Executive CV & Decks` link with `EN & AR` badge.
- **Hero CTAs:** Dual high-converting primary CTAs: `Explore Executive CV (EN/AR)` and `View 22-Slide Decks`.
- **GCC & Global Trust Pill:** Highlighting Riyadh, Saudi Arabia base and bilingual delivery capabilities.
